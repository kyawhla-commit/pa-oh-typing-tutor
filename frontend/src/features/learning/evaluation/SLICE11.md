4. **Architecture.** The adapter consumes the already-visible policy selection and exact executable action witnesses. The pure planner sequences compact references. The service validates freshness and delegates starts to existing selection/reservation code. Practice renders the intention and explicit start/continuation buttons. Policy decides recommendation priority, the generator supplies content, and the engine owns scoring.

5. **Input/output contracts.** Input: existing learner scope (up to the existing 512-unit storage bound), version/counter stamps, up to eight compact executable options, up to eight visible need identities, and three completed activity keys. Output: deeply immutable ID/scope/purpose/primary/nullable secondary/one-or-two activity estimate/explanation/evidence stamps/audit counters. Adaptive references contain source ID/version, current level/purpose/ordinal and verified length. No passage, prepared target, typed buffer, private source content or full history is retained. Plans are not persisted; a separate validated 4 KiB sidecar stores only three completion references per learner.

6. **Priority rules.** Executable official regression, then executable transfer check, then active needs in existing visible policy order and their already-authorized stage, then ordinary Practice. Targeted/contextual active actions share a sequencing tier to preserve recommendation ranking. Hidden audit rows are not revived; the accepted broad accuracy override remains authoritative. Normal fallback cannot displace an available focused priority action.

7. **Repetition policy.** After three consecutive matching completed action-type/weakness/purpose keys, use another executable option in the same priority tier, preferring an authorized same-weakness contextual alternative if supplied. Otherwise retain policy order. Tests/unknown sessions break the consecutive run. No alternative means repeat; no progression level or future schedule is invented. The production adapter emits only one current policy level per weakness.

8. **Availability and supporting activities.** Exact capability witnesses are projected without reserving cursors. Unavailable, malformed and provisionally mastered focused actions cannot be selected. Start revalidates full plan/scope/evidence/current ordinal before reservation. Ordinary Practice is the available host fallback. A supporting activity is ordinary Practice only: primary must be at most 180 verified graphemes, purpose must differ, and no unrelated visible need may compete, even if its focused action is unavailable. Continuation requires the launched primary's accepted completion and is consumed once. It is never queued or auto-started.

19. **Limitations.** Synthetic evidence establishes engineering behavior, not educational effectiveness. Diversity is limited to currently authorized visible options; no contextual alternative is fabricated. Finite English-bank reuse and Unicode drill limitations remain. Shortness is measured in graphemes, not predicted learner minutes. Only three completion references persist; reload discards an unfinished plan intention and generates a fresh plan. localStorage has no cross-tab transaction; missing/out-of-sync sidecars conservatively lose diversity history while preserving existing evidence/mastery. The benchmark reports sampled serialized state, not a heap-retention profile.

20. **Curriculum scheduling remains separate.** No spaced repetition, calendar, daily goals, reminders, streaks, automatic lesson queues, predictions, AI/LLM generation, telemetry or new cloud analytics were added.
