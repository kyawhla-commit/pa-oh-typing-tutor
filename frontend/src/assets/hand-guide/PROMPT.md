# Glass hand artwork provenance

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, using transparent background mode. Final asset: `glass-left-hand.png`. The second image was selected and copied into this project; the original outputs remain in the generated-images directory.

## Initial prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: transparent PNG overlay for a typing tutor keyboard. Create one exquisitely realistic anatomical LEFT human hand made from crystal clear translucent optical glass, viewed from above and slightly behind as if the learner is looking down at their own left hand resting naturally on a keyboard. No keyboard, no background, no text. Five fingers: little finger on LEFT, then ring, middle, index, thumb extends to RIGHT. Fingers slightly curved in a relaxed typing pose. FOUR fingertips must be nearly on one horizontal baseline, with the short little finger slightly lower. The hand fills a 1024 x 1536 portrait canvas: little fingertip approximately (175,260), ring (340,220), middle (505,200), index (675,225), thumb tip (845,750), wrist centered (435,1280) and continues toward the bottom. Keep 100 pixels padding around silhouette except wrist at bottom. Natural adult hand anatomy, subtle knuckles, tendon contours, soft realistic nail surfaces all made of transparent glass. Not a skeleton, not a robot, not cartoon or vector or wireframe. Smooth dimensional surface with physically realistic specular edge reflections. Extremely transparent low-opacity interior so letters under palm and fingers will remain legible. Almost colorless glass with soft pale ice-blue/cyan and faint violet reflections. Single subtle white studio reflection along each finger edge, polished clear optical glass, no bright glow. Real photographic-quality 3D product render, no opaque skin, no dark black reflections, no shadows on background, no props. Transparent background with genuine alpha channel. Do not color-code individual fingers; dynamic colors are added by the application.

## Refinement prompt used for the final asset

Edit this glass left hand asset for a keyboard typing guide. Keep the realistic transparent glass material, five natural fingers, translucent surface anatomy and portrait composition. Make the fingers curl naturally into a relaxed typing pose so the FOUR fingertips (little, ring, middle, index) end at almost exactly the SAME HORIZONTAL LINE y=300. Little tip x=180, ring x=340, middle x=505, index x=675. The four fingers should be foreshortened and bent over like typing onto the home row; their nails visible, no horizontal palm-facing grip. Thumb extends to right with its tip at x=845 y=700. Wrist toward bottom. Remove ALL ambient gray/black halo around the silhouette, remove any background, actual alpha zero outside hand. Very translucent pale ice-blue/clear glass, subtly sculpted contours, avoid thick opaque white edges or dark shadows. Keep light reflections realistic and fine. Camera looks slightly from behind, down onto the back of your left hand as it types. No keyboard and no text. 1024x1536 asset.

## Integration

The actual raster does not exactly match the requested coordinates and includes a soft halo. The component clips the silhouette, uses measured source fingertip anchors, independently fits the fingers to the rendered keys, applies transparency, and mirrors the image for the right hand. The asset is decorative and conveys no keyboard mapping by itself.
